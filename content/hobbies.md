## Basketball

<img src="/media/hobbies/basketball/4.png">

*Standing with my team and coach, as the proud captain of our first inter-IIT Gold winning team (after 57 years)*

Basketball has been a key part of my life, ever since childhood. It has taught me discipline - waking up at 6 am for pratice in the winters, how to push myself - when you think you can't possibly run anymore, running that extra lap and teamwork - how to understand different people, communicate effectively and come together to achieve a common goal. These learnings form the basis of my character on and even off court.

## Event Organization

<img src="/media/hobbies/event_organization/11.png">

*Organizing a murder mystery themed scavenger hunt (as a freshman) for over 100 participants*

I enjoy organizing events and competitions that bring people together through teamwork, strategy, and curiosity.
